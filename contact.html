Helpful support requests

What to include

Tell us your Android version, VEXO Hub version, the screen you were using, what you expected and what happened. Screenshots are useful when they do not reveal private information.

Protect your information

Never send your password, authentication token, unredacted identity document, private backup or checksum file by email. VEXO Hub support will not ask for your password.
