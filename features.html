Product features

Everything you need to stay organized

Capture important information, give it structure and retrieve it quickly—without turning your organization system into another source of complexity.

Capture

Smart Notes

Save the information that matters with a title, category, notes and optional structured details. VEXO Hub keeps each record clear and easy to revisit.

Store

Documents and Images

Connect photos and files to the item they explain—whether that is a receipt, policy, reference image or maintenance document.

Remember

Reminders

Schedule important dates for renewals, warranties, maintenance and personal events. Notification preferences remain in your control.

Organize

Custom Categories

Start with useful everyday categories, then add, reorder, archive or restore categories as your organization system evolves.

Retrieve

Fast Search

Search records by title and narrow results by category so important information does not disappear inside a large archive.

Prioritize

Favorites

Mark high-value records as favorites and keep the information you use most in a dedicated view.

Protect

Backup and Restore

Create a local backup with an integrity checksum, export both files to secure storage and restore a validated backup when needed.

Access

Offline-First Workspace

Core organization features use a local workspace, keeping your information available when connectivity is limited or unavailable.
